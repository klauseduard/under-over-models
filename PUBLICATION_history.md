### Edition 2025 (Historical Snapshot)

**Published:** April–May 2025  
**Git tag:** `release-2025`

The initial publication exploring how underfitting and overfitting concepts from machine learning apply to software development estimation, organizational design, and AI transformation.

- 🚧 Chapter 8: AI Organizational Impact (Draft)
- 🚧 Chapter 9: AI: Theory and Practice (Draft)
- 🚧 Chapter 10: Conclusion (Draft)

**Historical note:** The 2025 edition was preserved as a historical snapshot to enable future analysis of how well its predictions — particularly regarding AI's organizational impact (Chapters 8–9) — held up over time. An independent assessment conducted in March 2026 found that the core analytical framework and many specific predictions aged well, while the pace of AI agent adoption and the disproportionate impact on entry-level workers exceeded what the edition anticipated. See the 2026 edition's Chapter 10 for a retrospective analysis.

### Authorship

This publication is directed and authored by a human practitioner with AI tools (Claude, Gemini) assisting in text generation, research, evidence gathering, and visualization creation. The human author is responsible for the conceptual direction, knowledge contributions, editorial decisions, and quality control.
